## Sandboxels
<iframe src="https://turbowarp.org/1090840583/embed" width="482" height="412" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" allowfullscreen="" style="color-scheme: auto"></iframe>

This is a recreation of the game sandboxels that I made.
- Space to reset
- 1 to pause
- 0 to show/hide variables
- Hold the mouse to draw water

- Press the following buttons while holding mouse to draw other materials:
	- a to erase
	- b to draw stone
	- c to draw floaters
	- d to draw fire
	- e to draw ash
	- f to draw sand
	- g to draw plants
	- h to draw gas
	- i to draw dead Conway's tiles
	- j to draw live Conway's tiles
	- k to draw seeds
	- l to draw lava
	- m to draw acid
	- n to draw wet sand
	- o to draw wood
	- p to draw base
